Keep what you're working on in front of you.

You start something, switch to something else, and forget the first thing exists until tomorrow. MultiTasker pins every task you've started in a small widget at the top-right of your screen, so nothing in progress slips out of sight.

Download for Windows See the source

Free for Windows 10 and 11. Installs without admin rights and keeps itself up to date.

It notices what you're on

Link the files, folders, apps and web pages a task uses. When one of them is in front, that task shows as active now, and after a short wait it starts on its own. You never have to remember to press start.

Step away from the computer and your tasks pause by themselves, so a lunch break or a night's sleep never counts as work.

Pick up where you left off

Pausing asks for one line on where you stopped. Starting again shows that line and offers to open everything the task uses in one go: its files, its folders, and its pages, switching to browser tabs you already have open instead of opening them twice.

Waiting on something? Get on with something else

Tell MultiTasker what you're waiting for: a build in a terminal, a render, an export, a download. It pauses the task and lets you know the moment it's done, with a button straight back to it.

Know when the AI has answered

Long replies from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI chats take a while. With the browser extension, you can ask, go back to your work, and get a note when the reply has finished, if you've moved to another tab.

Link the page you're on

The extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave adds the tab in front to any task, or starts a new task from it. Stay on an unlinked page for a while with a task in progress, and the widget offers to link it for you.

Plan the day, then see where it went

  • Pick what today is for each morning; those tasks stay at the top of the list.
  • Break tasks into checklists, and see the next step right in the widget.
  • Add due times with reminders, and get a nudge for anything due soon that hasn't been started.
  • See how long each task took, today and this week.
  • Hand in finished work: MultiTasker keeps a copy of every version and checks it's ready first.

Your tasks stay where they already are

Keep tasks on your computer, or use the ones you already have in Todoist or a Notion database. Working on more than one computer? MultiTasker syncs through OneDrive, Google Drive, a shared folder or S3-compatible storage that you choose, and your settings follow along.

There's no MultiTasker account and no MultiTasker server. Read the privacy policy.
